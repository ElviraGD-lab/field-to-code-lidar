---
title: "From Field to Code: LiDAR from Scratch"
---

Work with real point clouds and learn to think like an engineer.

This book follows a single real problem from start to finish: how fast the forest grows next to the power lines of the Pajares pass, measured with the national LiDAR surveys of Spain (PNOA). Along the way you'll learn what a LiDAR measures, how to read a point cloud and how to work with PDAL, but above all you'll see how an engineer thinks: how to frame the question, check every step and know when a result can be trusted. In the end the work becomes an open tool: [`canopy-growth`](https://github.com/ElviraGD-lab/canopy-growth).

*Work in progress. Chapters are published as they are written.*
