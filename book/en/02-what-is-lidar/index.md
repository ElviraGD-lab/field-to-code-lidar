---
title: "Chapter 2. What LiDAR is"
---

In this chapter we get to grips with the instrument itself: how it measures distance, how a distance becomes a point with coordinates, where survey strips come from, what LiDAR sees in a forest, and which parts of its data we can trust. There's not much code here, but there's plenty of working things out by hand. Without that, we won't be able to understand where the errors in our numbers come from.
